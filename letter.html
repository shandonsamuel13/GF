A Letter, Just For You

A

tap the envelope to open it

My Anuksha,

If today is about celebrating you, then I want to start by celebrating the very ordinary things — the way you laugh before the joke is even finished, the way you remember details about people you met once, the way a room settles when you walk into it.

Twenty-two years of you have made this world quietly, thoroughly better, and I feel unreasonably lucky to be one of the people who gets to watch it happen up close.

You are patient with people who don't deserve it, kind in ways you never announce, and stronger than you give yourself credit for on the days that ask the most of you. I notice all of it, even when I don't say so.

So here's to another year of your terrible puns, your very good taste, your late-night ambitions, and every plan we haven't made yet. I'll be right here for it — all of it.

Happy birthday, my love.

Always yours
